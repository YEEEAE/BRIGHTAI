Three Levels of Automation — What's the Diff?

Traditional Automation (RPA)

Executes fixed tasks with rigid rules. Good for basic ops, but if an exception pops up, it freezes and needs a human. It runs on basic scripts, not a high-end ai software.

Smart Automation (AI Automation)

Mixes AI with your workflow to understand context and handle messy data — like reading PDF invoices or sorting customer messages. It's like having an ai bot or enterprise ai tools handling exceptions on their own. This is the level BrightAI delivers.

Hyperautomation

The absolute peak — fusing RPA, AI, NLP, and ML into one beast of an ecosystem that runs itself. It spots processes to automate, builds tracks, and measures ROI. Totally elite for major Saudi enterprises looking for a high-impact saas.
