        Does the system make autonomous hiring decisions?
        
      No. Bright AI behaves as a decision-support mechanism. It organizes unstructured resumes into structured evidence-based dashboards. Final candidate selections remain completely human-driven.
      
      
      
      
      
        Does the engine evaluate personal demographic characteristics?
        
      No. Strict system directives enforce the absolute exclusion of name, age, gender, photos, marital status, and nationality to avoid automated profiling and bias.
      
      
      
      
      
        Can this be customized to our internal evaluation framework?
        
      Yes. The enterprise licensing model supports custom evaluation variables, direct ATS integrations, and localized hosting setups to match your company's competency frameworks.
