Frequently Asked Questions

        
Does it support conversational questions?
Yes. The interface accepts conversational prompts (including mixed Arabic/English), parsing them instantly into queries or statistics.

        
Does DuckDB execute client-side?
Yes. It runs DuckDB-WASM inside your browser for ultra-fast, local data manipulation, falling back gracefully to JS if needed.

        
Can we export the generated charts?
Yes. Every visualization supports PNG downloads, iframe embed codes, and an AI narrative generator.

        
Do you use reasoning models?
The default is Gemini 2.5 Flash, with the option to toggle Gemini 2.5 Flash Thinking for complex multi-layered data audits.
