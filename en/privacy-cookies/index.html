What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us deliver a better browsing experience and understand how visitors use our site. Bright AI, as a leading AI company in Saudi Arabia, uses cookies responsibly to improve your experience.

Types of Cookies We Use

Saudi Compliance & Regulations

Our Commitment to Protecting Your Data — Bright AI Comprehensive Privacy Policy

At Bright AI, we fully understand that data is the most valuable asset in the modern digital age. As a leading AI company in Saudi Arabia providing AI automation Saudi Arabia and Enterprise AI Saudi Arabia solutions, we do not merely deliver advanced technology — we build our solutions on a foundation of transparency, respect for user privacy, and unwavering commitment to best practices.

1 Compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

Our operational and technical policies are designed for full compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) enacted in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. As a trusted AI company in Saudi Arabia, we strictly adhere to the core principles of the law, including:

  • Lawful Purpose: Data is collected for a specific, clear purpose and is not processed for other uses without prior consent.
  • Data Minimisation: We collect only the minimum necessary operational information to deliver AI and AI automation Saudi Arabia services successfully.
  • Sharing Restrictions: We do not sell, lease, or share customer databases or log files with any external third parties for commercial purposes unless required by regulation.

2 National Data Sovereignty & Cloud Hosting

In response to directives from the National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) and the National Data Management Office (NDMO), we offer our clients in sensitive sectors — such as healthcare using Smart Medical Archive or financial institutions — fully local cloud hosting options.

This ensures that all customer inputs, training data, and knowledge bases uploaded to Bright AI agents remain encrypted end-to-end (E2EE) and stored exclusively within geographically approved servers inside Saudi Arabia, preventing uncontrolled cross-border data transfer.

3 How We Process Your Data in AI Models

A critical question many enterprises ask: Will our private data and interactions be used to train large language models (LLMs) for other parties?

At Bright AI, the answer is clear: No.

Data you enter through our platforms — whether customer inquiries in BrightSupport or task reports in BrightProject — is logically and physically isolated per tenant (tenant isolation). Our AI models use a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture, meaning the system retrieves context only from your dedicated knowledge base at query time. The core model weights are never updated with your conversations, ensuring zero data leakage to other users.

4 Your Rights as a User or Corporate Representative (Data Subject Rights)

We guarantee transparent commitment and direct empowerment over your digital footprint. In accordance with local regulations, our clients hold the following rights:

  • Right to Know: Request a detailed report on how your demographic and technical data is collected and processed.
  • Right to Rectification: Contact us to update inaccurate or outdated organisational information.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Any entity may request the complete and secure deletion of its data from our servers after the contract term ends, following a strict security policy that ensures irreversible erasure.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Opt out of data analysis for marketing purposes at any time.

5 Proactive Security Measures

To protect our digital environment and client records, Bright AI implements robust security protocols including Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for our staff, AES-256 database encryption, and daily vulnerability scanning and penetration testing by independent agencies to prevent and track phishing attempts or unauthorised access.
