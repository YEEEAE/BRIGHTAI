Pricing & Money Talk 💰

How much does this stuff cost?

Real talk – it depends on what you need. A simple automation might cost way less than a full AI platform. That's why we start with a free consultation to understand your situation and give you a quote that actually makes sense for your budget.

Is the first consultation really free?

100% free. No strings attached. We'll hop on a call, hear you out, understand your challenges, and give you an honest assessment. If we're a good fit, cool. If not, no hard feelings. We keep it real. ✌️

How long do projects usually take?

It really depends on the complexity. Simple projects might take 2-4 weeks. More complex enterprise solutions could take 2-3 months. We'll always give you a realistic timeline upfront – no cap.

Security & Privacy 🔐

Is my data safe with you?

Security is literally our top priority. All data is hosted locally in Saudi Arabia with AES-256 encryption. We comply with NCA (National Cybersecurity Authority) and NDMO standards. Your data stays safe, stays local, stays private. Period.

Where do you host the data?

Everything is hosted locally within Saudi Arabia. This ensures data sovereignty and compliance with local regulations. No offshore servers, no sketchy third parties. Just secure, local hosting.
