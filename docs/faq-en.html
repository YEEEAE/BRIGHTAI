General Stuff

What exactly is BrightAI?

We're a Saudi-based AI company that helps businesses level up with smart tech. Think automation, data analytics, chatbots, and custom AI solutions. Our squad of 11 experts has shipped 47 projects and counting. We're basically your tech homies who actually deliver. 🤙

Where are you guys based?

We're in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia! 🇸🇦 Specifically at: 6913 Al Mubarak Bin Fadalah, Al Fayha District, Riyadh 14254. But hey, we work with clients from all over KSA – most of our collabs happen over video calls anyway.

Do you work with clients outside Riyadh?

For sure! We serve clients across all of Saudi Arabia. Most of our communication happens remotely through video calls, but if you need us on-site, we can make that happen too. Distance ain't an issue.

Services

What services do you offer?

We've got a full stack of AI goodies:

Can you build custom solutions?

Absolutely! That's actually our specialty. Every project we take on is tailored to your specific needs. We don't do cookie-cutter solutions – we build stuff that actually fits your business like a glove. 🧤

Pricing & Money Talk 💰

How much does this stuff cost?

Real talk – it depends on what you need. A simple automation might cost way less than a full AI platform. That's why we start with a free consultation to understand your situation and give you a quote that actually makes sense for your budget.

Is the first consultation really free?

100% free. No strings attached. We'll hop on a call, hear you out, understand your challenges, and give you an honest assessment. If we're a good fit, cool. If not, no hard feelings. We keep it real. ✌️

How long do projects usually take?

It really depends on the complexity. Simple projects might take 2-4 weeks. More complex enterprise solutions could take 2-3 months. We'll always give you a realistic timeline upfront – no cap.

Security & Privacy 🔐

Is my data safe with you?

Security is literally our top priority. All data is hosted locally in Saudi Arabia with AES-256 encryption. We comply with NCA (National Cybersecurity Authority) and NDMO standards. Your data stays safe, stays local, stays private. Period.

Where do you host the data?

Everything is hosted locally within Saudi Arabia. This ensures data sovereignty and compliance with local regulations. No offshore servers, no sketchy third parties. Just secure, local hosting.

Support

We don't ghost. Ever. We provide ongoing support 24/7 in both Arabic and English. Whether it's WhatsApp or email, we're always just a message away. Your success is our success.
