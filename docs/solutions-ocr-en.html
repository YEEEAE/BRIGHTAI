More Than Just a Text Reader

Traditional OCR struggles with Arabic cursive fonts or tilted documents. But **Bright OCR** uses Deep Learning (Computer Vision) to understand the document structure, not just the letters.

Our system knows the difference between "Invoice Date" and "Due Date", and extracts tables (Line Items) with extreme accuracy, making it perfect for accounting, archiving, and legal work.

When is OCR and intelligent document processing the right fit?

This page should make OCR easier to understand for buyers: it is best used when teams handle large volumes of invoices, forms, IDs, PDFs, or scanned operational documents that must become searchable, structured, and usable in downstream systems.

Inputs

Scans, PDFs, invoices, IDs, forms, and document-heavy workflows.

Outputs

Digital text, extracted fields, document classification, and structured records.

Business value

Less manual entry, faster review cycles, and stronger traceability across teams.

Direct use cases and value

  • Useful for finance, archives, healthcare, logistics, and service operations.
  • Turns static documents into operational data.
  • Works best when connected to approval logic and internal systems.
