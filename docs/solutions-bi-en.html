Is Data "Dry"? We Make It Speak!

The problem for managers today isn't a lack of data, but "Data Overload". Reports from accounting, marketing, numbers in the system... and the challenge is how to connect them.

Bright BI services take all this chaos and organize it into beautiful, easy screens. Instead of reading 50 pages, you see a red indicator alerting you: "Sales in Jeddah branch dropped, and the reason is low stock". That's the "gist" every manager needs.

FAQ

Do you support Arabic in Power BI?

Yes, all our dashboard designs fully support Arabic (RTL) with Saudi number and date formatting.

Can it connect to local accounting software?

Absolutely. We have extensive experience connecting via API with accounting systems like Daftra, Qoyod, and Zoho, as well as global systems like SAP and Oracle.

How long does it take to build a custom dashboard?

It depends on data volume and complexity, but we typically deliver the initial version (MVP) within two weeks of receiving data access.
