📜 Focus ERP: M.Prod Rejected Stock Views

The following reject views are configured in Focus ERP for production and warehouse use:

  • Rejection of Semi-Finished — In-process production rejects
  • Rejection of Finished Goods — Final product quality failures
  • Rejection of Injection — Injection molding defects
  • Injection Operational Waste — Startup/scrap from injection machines
  • Rejection of Raw Materials — Incoming material failures
  • Sponge / Foam Rejects — Color change and degradation
  • Packaging Rejects — Label misprints, damaged packaging
  • QC Sample Hold — Samples pending lab results

Responsibility: Production supervisors and warehouse operators enter reject data directly into Focus ERP at the time of detection. QC Compliance verifies and controls.

⚠ Advisory: Machine quality indicators are simulated for demonstration. Actual machine data must be integrated from Focus ERP or the production monitoring system. AI recommendations are advisory and require formal review.
