Finance Review Logic

Cost Threshold Concept: Items with reject cost exceeding SAR 5,000 are flagged for finance review. Critical-risk items above SAR 20,000 require Finance Director sign-off before destruction.

Recovery Evaluation Workflow:

  1. QC confirms the rejection reason and quality status.
  2. Finance evaluates potential recovery options (rework, return, downgrade).
  3. If no recovery is feasible, Finance recommends destruction.
  4. QCM approves the quality decision in Focus ERP.
  5. Finance records the loss and initiates destruction workflow.
