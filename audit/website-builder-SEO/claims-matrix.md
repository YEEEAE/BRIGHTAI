## Claim-risk summary

- **High risk:** The existing entity schema carries address, geo, service-area, hours, payment, price-range, founder, and founding-date data that this phase could not independently verify. Treat those fields as review-required before relying on them for search or conversion decisions.
- **High risk:** Regulatory names may appear only as educational context or readiness support. They must not become a claim of legal compliance, certification, or regulator approval.
- **High risk:** Existing contact CTAs are not proof of an operational conversion system. The owner must name the conversion, destination, owner, and privacy/consent behavior.
- **Identity risk:** Until the legal entity and delivery model are confirmed, visitors could mistake BrightAI for a government authority, certification provider, auditor, legal adviser, or a fully automated security guarantee. The limitation language must remain prominent wherever the offer is described.
