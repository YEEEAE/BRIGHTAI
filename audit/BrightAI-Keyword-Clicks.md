## Important note about Clicks

`Clicks` below means **actual search clicks per query from Google Search Console**, not Search Volume and not estimated traffic.

When a GSC Performance export/API dataset becomes available, replace `N/A` with the actual click count for the same reporting period and add `Impressions`, `CTR`, `Average Position`, `Start Date`, and `End Date`.

## Data Rules

- Search Volume is not Clicks.
- `0` Search Volume means the keyword tool reported zero for the selected Saudi market/language at the time of research; it does not prove nobody searches the topic.
- `N/A` means the metric was not available from the connected/retrieved source.
- Never convert Search Volume into "actual clicks" without a clearly labeled estimation model.
- For business decisions, prioritize qualified leads and commercial intent over raw volume.
