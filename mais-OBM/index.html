30 Mar 2026 Investigated ✓

Abnormally High Rejection Rate — BU-093-1000 (Laryngeal Airway Mask Size 1)

During OBM sampling on 30 March, product BU-093-1000 (Laryngeal Airway Mask Size 1 PVC) recorded rejection rates of 21.72%, 5.36%, 55.73%, and 66.67% across multiple inspection records — the highest in this reporting period.

Root Cause: Investigation confirmed that this product batch (Lot: CD14J03) is extremely old stock dating back to prior years. Quality degradation over time — including material brittleness, discoloration, and cuff integrity failures — led to mass rejection during sampling.

Additionally, 2024 quality audit records had already flagged concerns about this batch's storage conditions and aging profile.

30 Mar 2026 Resolved ✓

Unauthorized Stock Transfer — BU-093-1000

It was discovered that the entire quantity of BU-093-1000 (1,682 units across two lots) was physically transferred from the warehouse to the production floor without any formal transfer documentation — no transfer orders were created, and no entries were recorded in the FOCUS ERP system.

The product was found physically present on the production line but had no traceability trail in the system. Investigation determined this was an individual human error by a warehouse operator who bypassed standard procedures.

Resolution: The issue has been fully resolved. Proper transfer documents have been retroactively created, inventory records corrected in FOCUS, and the responsible personnel has been counseled. A reminder on SOPs for stock movements has been circulated to all warehouse staff.

07-08 Mar 2026 Investigated ✓

Elevated Rejections — Laryngeal Airway Masks (Sizes 4.0 & 5.0)

Products BU-093-4000 (Size 4.0) and BU-093-5000 (Size 5.0) showed rejection rates of 30.64% and 24.85% respectively during OBM sampling on 7-8 March 2026.

These rates are significantly above the standard threshold. The batch (Lot: 231028) has been flagged for detailed quality review. Preliminary findings suggest potential supplier-side quality issues from the 2023 procurement batch.
